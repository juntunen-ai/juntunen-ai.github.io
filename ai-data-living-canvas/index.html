03 · Translate capability into outcomes

Technology is not the value.

The value comes from what the business can now do reliably, faster, or at scale.

01Business continuity

Less dependence on manual work, single experts and fragile handovers.

02Customer value

03Efficiency

04Confidence

Better traceability, stronger evidence and earlier risk identification.

How might we measure progress?

Candidate KPIs for a pilot; establish baselines with customer data before setting targets.

Hours per reporting cycle

Manual collection, mapping and reconciliation time.

Exceptions requiring review

Number and severity of unresolved issues.

Evidence completeness

Share of material claims linked to verified source records.

The idea to remember
AI does not make data true. It can make trustworthy data easier to find, align, verify and put to work.
